How I work

01

Start with the learner

Who are they, what's their day like, what do they already know, and where will they be when they need this? You can't know what someone needs until you know who they are.

02

Ask what's actually breaking

Most "training problems" are process problems wearing a costume. I talk to the people doing the work before I scope a single screen.

03

Design for the hardest case first

Keyboard only, screen reader, no audio, low bandwidth, interrupted twice. If it works there it works everywhere — that's universal design, not a checklist.
