# Universal Design as a Technical Writing Approach With over a decade of educational and professional experience in technical writing and editing, I've found significant overlap between Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles and technical documentation principles: For instance, both UDL and technical writing value the use of plain language. This overlap makes sense when considering the core purpose of both UDL and technical writing: To educate. UDL removes barriers in the learning process by ensuring equitable access and opportunities for learning. Similarly, technical writers break down complex information into simple language for any user, regardless of technical knowledge. When combined, UDL and technical writing principles can produce superior technical documentation.
